### Added
- Accents are optional when you search: “cafe” finds “Café”, “Lodz” finds “Łódź”, “strasse” finds “Straße”, in search, quick open and links (`[[Lodz]]` opens `Łódź.md` when nothing matches exactly). Highlights land on the text as written. The find bar has a “Match accents” toggle (off by default); Find and replace in all notes has a “Match accents” checkbox (on, as before).
- Ask your notes says “Not found in your notes” when your notes don't answer the question, and shows the passages that came closest, instead of quoting something unrelated.
- Ask cites the exact sentences an answer comes from, not the whole section, and lists them under the answer.
- **Save as note** in Ask: the answer becomes a note in `Answers/` (or `wiki/answers/` when the vault has an agent `wiki` folder) with the question, the date, who answered, an “AI wrote this” callout, and each source as a footnote quoting the cited text.
- Quire notices when a passage a saved answer quotes changes or disappears: the footnote is marked, a note under it shows what the text says now with **Update citation** and **Open**, a bar above the note says how many need a look, and the Inbox lists them under Citations. This works offline.
- **Check citations in this note**: with an AI model set up (and Settings → Trust → “Check citations with the AI model” on), the model says whether each cited passage supports the sentence citing it; ones that don't are listed in the Inbox. Without a model, citations that share few words with their sentence are pointed out in the note.
- **Say what you expect first** (Settings → Trust, or per question in Ask): write what you think the answer is, see the passages grouped by topic, then the answer, which says where your notes agree or disagree with you.
- **Getting stale**: a weekly digest in the Inbox of notes that may be out of date (old and not reviewed, linked notes that changed a lot since, words like “currently” or “as of”, a past `valid_until:` or a `superseded_by:`), each with the reasons, **Mark reviewed** (sets `reviewed:` to today) and Snooze. Set how long a folder stays fresh in `.quire/shared/vault.toml` under `[staleness]`, or per note with `review_every: 90d`. Also the command “Mark note as reviewed”.
- **Find contradictions** (Preview, off by default; Settings → Trust): an AI model lists the claims in your notes and compares claims about the same thing. Pairs that disagree appear in the Inbox with **Open both** (side by side, the passages highlighted), **Mark superseded**, **Ask agent to reconcile** and Dismiss. Before it starts it says how many requests the first run takes; cloud requests stay within a daily limit (200 by default) and it runs only while you're not typing. Results are cached, so a passage or a pair is sent once.
- **Thinking timeline** (Preview; right panel, command “Thinking timeline…”): for a tag, a note or a few words, when your notes started saying something, when they took it back and when another note contradicted it, read from the notes' saved versions. Offline it reads plain sentences (“approximate”); “Fill in with AI” asks the model about older versions, within a request limit.
- Search by section (agents' `search` tool): results are the matching section with its line range, filtered by folder, tags, a view condition (`status != done and author.name = Ada`), modified dates, and PDF and image text when asked for.

### Changed
- Find in note ignores accents unless “Match accents” is on.
- Ask your notes opens as its own dialog and remembers your question until you ask another.
